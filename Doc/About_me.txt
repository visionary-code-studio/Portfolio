# 25 — Content Writing Guidelines

## Tone

The portfolio should sound:

```text
Personal
Confident
Curious
Intelligent
Approachable
Authentic
```

Avoid:

```text
Corporate clichés
Overly exaggerated claims
Generic AI-generated phrases
Long paragraphs
Unverifiable achievements
```

## Preferred Writing Style

Instead of:

> I am a highly motivated and passionate individual who possesses a strong desire to learn and grow.

Prefer:

> I like building things, understanding how they work, and learning what I don't know yet.
